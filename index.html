The JCAP Bioinformatics Platform

From data to a report you can share with your PI

Numbers. Narrative. Next Steps.

JCAP connects gene-expression analysis, biological interpretation, scientific papers, and possible follow-up experiments. Instead of leaving you with only spreadsheets and plots, it helps prepare a report you can review with your principal investigator (PI).

01 · Numbers

Find important changes in genes and biological pathways, backed by statistics.

02 · Narrative

Explain what the results may mean and connect them to published research.

03 · Next Steps

Suggest questions and follow-up experiments, assembled into a reviewable research report.

What can JCAP analyze?

Bulk RNA-seq: compare gene activity between groups of samples. Single-cell RNA-seq: see how individual cell types respond. Perturb-seq: explore how changing a gene affects cells.

What will I see in the dashboard?

Analysis results, interactive plots, cell-state information (where relevant), gene-pathway networks, summaries of research papers, and a final report—all organized by stage.

What changes in a tumor?

JCAP found changes related to the tissue surrounding breast tumors, consistent with established breast-cancer biology.

Single-cell RNA-seq · Immune response

How do immune cells respond?

JCAP found the expected antiviral response in immune cells exposed to interferon-beta, matching the main biology of the original study.

The Kang benchmark recovered interferon-response genes and pathways. Cell-level and donor-aware pseudobulk analyses agreed on the dominant antiviral response. Pseudobulk means grouping cells by donor so people—not individual cells—provide the biological replication.

Dashboard integration and performance testing

We reviewed the integrated dashboard and optimized it to use the existing end-to-end pipelines rather than duplicating analysis through individual tools. The output tabs were also reorganized for more efficient navigation and review of results. We also provided for one-click demos with testing data.

During testing, we observed that very large network graphs can require substantial time to render in the dashboard UI. For highly complex enrichment datasets, we therefore recommend using the underlying CLI/Nextflow pipelines for primary analysis unless sufficient computational resources are available for interactive visualization. This rendering limitation does not affect the underlying network analysis, which is completed separately from UI visualization.

Also tested: the airway bulk RNA-seq sample included in the repository. Benchmark results show recovery of selected published biological findings; they do not guarantee accuracy for every dataset. Interpretation and proposed experiments require scientific review.
